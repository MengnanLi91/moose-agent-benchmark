# Problem specification

On $\Omega=[0,1]^2$ for $0\le t\le1$, consider

$$
\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}-\nabla^2u=s.
$$

Use

$$
u^*(x,y,t)=(1+t)\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi y).
$$

Derive and report:

1. $\partial u^*/\partial t$ and $\nabla^2u^*$;
2. the source $s(x,y,t)$;
3. the Dirichlet data on all four sides;
4. the initial condition at $t=0$;
5. a substitution demonstrating residual closure.
