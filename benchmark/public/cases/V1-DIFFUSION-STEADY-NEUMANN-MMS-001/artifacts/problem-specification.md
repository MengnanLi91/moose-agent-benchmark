# Problem specification

On $\Omega=[0,1]^2$, consider

$$
-\nabla^2u=s, \qquad \nabla u\cdot\mathbf n=g \text{ on }\partial\Omega.
$$

Use

$$
u^*(x,y)=\cos(\pi x)\cos(\pi y)+2.
$$

Derive and report:

1. the gradient and Laplacian of $u^*$;
2. the source $s(x,y)$;
3. the outward-normal flux $g$ on all four sides;
4. the domain mean used to remove the Neumann nullspace;
5. the integral compatibility condition and its value for this MMS.
