# Canonical derivation

For

$$
u^*=\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi y),
$$

$$
u_x^*=\pi\cos(\pi x)\sin(\pi y),\qquad
u_y^*=\pi\sin(\pi x)\cos(\pi y),
$$

and

$$
u_{xx}^*=u_{yy}^*=-\pi^2\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi y).
$$

Therefore

$$
\nabla^2u^*=-2\pi^2\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi y),
\qquad
s=-\nabla^2u^*=2\pi^2\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi y).
$$

The exact field is zero on $x=0$, $x=1$, $y=0$, and $y=1$, so homogeneous Dirichlet data close the manufactured problem.
