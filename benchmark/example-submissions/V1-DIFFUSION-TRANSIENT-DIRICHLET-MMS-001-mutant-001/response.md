# Canonical derivation

For

$$
u^*=(1+t)\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi y),
$$

$$
\nabla^2u^*=-2\pi^2(1+t)\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi y).
$$

Substitution into $u_t-\nabla^2u=s$ gives

$$
s=\left[2\pi^2(1+t)\right]\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi y).
$$

The field is zero on all four boundaries. At $t=0$ the required initial condition is

$$
u(x,y,0)=\sin(\pi x)\sin(\pi y).
$$
