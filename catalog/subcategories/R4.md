# R4 — Initialization and step-control repair

## Capability

Repair initial states, continuation strategy, timestep control, or nonlinear step management.

## Isolation guidance

Supply accepted equations, discretization, and solver structure. Keep the primary defect in
initialization or step control.

## Review questions

- Does the repair preserve the requested physical problem?
- Are attempt budgets and terminal reasons recorded?
