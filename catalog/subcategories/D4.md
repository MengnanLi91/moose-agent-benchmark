# D4 — Discriminating next test

## Capability

Choose a bounded diagnostic action that best separates the remaining plausible causes.

## Isolation guidance

Supply a constrained hypothesis set and available diagnostic actions. Do not score eventual
repair success.

## Review questions

- Does the proposed test distinguish plausible causes rather than merely gather more data?
- Are cost and action permissions explicit?
