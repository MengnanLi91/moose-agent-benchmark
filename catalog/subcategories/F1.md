# F1 — Physics specification and constitutive closure

## Capability

State the governing physics, variables, constitutive laws, domains, and closure assumptions.

## Isolation guidance

Supply geometry and problem objectives. Do not require weak-form derivation, MOOSE object
selection, or input syntax for full credit.

## Review questions

- Are all required constitutive dependencies objectively gradable?
- Can a correct physics specification receive full credit without downstream implementation?
