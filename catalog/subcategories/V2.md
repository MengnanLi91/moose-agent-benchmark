# V2 — Discretization-error estimation

## Capability

Estimate spatial or temporal discretization error using a defensible refinement study.

## Isolation guidance

Supply an accepted model and QoI definition. Exclude unrelated model calibration.

## Review questions

- Are refinement ratios and asymptotic-regime requirements explicit?
- Are reported orders and uncertainty recomputed privately?
