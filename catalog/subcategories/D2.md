# D2 — Causal-chain reconstruction

## Capability

Connect observed evidence through intermediate effects to the terminal failure.

## Isolation guidance

Supply the failure stage or prevent stage classification from dominating. Do not require a
repair patch.

## Review questions

- Is the accepted causal sequence supported by supplied evidence?
- Are alternative chains handled without rewarding unsupported speculation?
