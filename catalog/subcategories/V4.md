# V4 — External evidence comparison

## Capability

Compare simulation quantities of interest with trusted analytical, experimental, or
high-fidelity reference evidence.

## Isolation guidance

Supply provenance, units, alignment rules, and uncertainty information for the reference.

## Review questions

- Are comparison quantities and transformations unambiguous?
- Are uncertainty and applicability limits represented in the conclusion?
