# R3 — Solver, scaling, and nullspace repair

## Capability

Restore convergence by correcting solver structure, scaling, preconditioning, or nullspace
handling.

## Isolation guidance

Supply an accepted discretization and executable model with no hidden physics defect.

## Review questions

- Is convergence failure attributable to the targeted solver mechanism?
- Are success and resource limits measured by private execution?
