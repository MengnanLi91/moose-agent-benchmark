# D1 — Failure-stage classification

## Capability

Identify the earliest workflow stage that fails and distinguish it from later symptoms.

## Isolation guidance

Supply immutable artifacts and sufficient evidence. Repair and exact root-cause localization
are not required.

## Review questions

- Is the earliest failed stage objectively determined?
- Are misleading terminal symptoms separated from stage classification?
