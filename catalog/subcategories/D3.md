# D3 — Root-cause localization

## Capability

Name the cause family and locate the faulty block, object, parameter, variable, or equation
term.

## Isolation guidance

Supply or constrain failure-stage and causal-chain information. Repair is not scored.

## Review questions

- Is there one independent primary root cause?
- Is the target location objectively gradable from artifact-bound evidence?
